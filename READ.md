# 🎓 SyncLecture AI — Assistant & Section Tracker for Lecturers

SyncLecture AI eliminates cross-section confusion and administrative drag for college professors who teach multiple sections of the same course. Instead of manual note-taking, professors record a 30-second post-class voice memo. Gemini AI automatically parses topics covered, logs homework, highlights student friction points, and delivers a next-class pre-flight briefing straight to the professor's email.
